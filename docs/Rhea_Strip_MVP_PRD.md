# Rhea Strip
## Yield Tokenization & Interest Rate Derivatives Protocol on NEAR

> **The first yield-tokenization and interest-rate-derivatives protocol on NEAR — separating yield-bearing assets into tradable Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT).**

**Product Proposal · MVP PRD**

---

## 1. Project Overview

Rhea Strip is a yield-tokenization and interest-rate-derivatives protocol on NEAR that separates yield-bearing assets—including Meta Pool stNEAR, LiNEAR, RHEA LP, and cross-chain RWA such as USDY and BUIDL via NEAR Intents—into **Principal Tokens (PT)** and **Yield Tokens (YT)**, enabling principal and yield to be traded independently.

Rhea Strip transforms yield from a passive component of asset ownership into a financial instrument that can be separated, priced, and traded. The protocol establishes a foundation for fixed-yield positioning, yield speculation, and forward-selling future yield.

## 2. Problem Statement

Yield-bearing assets combine principal exposure and future yield in a single position. This limits users' ability to manage either component independently.

Users need ways to:

- Separate principal from future yield into distinct financial instruments.
- Trade principal and yield exposures independently.
- Access fixed-yield opportunities through discounted PT positions.
- Take positions on future yield through YT.
- Sell future yield upfront to unlock liquidity before maturity.

Rhea Strip addresses these limitations by introducing yield stripping and independent PT/YT markets to NEAR.

## 3. Product Vision

Rhea Strip aims to become a foundational infrastructure layer for yield tokenization and interest-rate derivatives in the NEAR ecosystem.

### Core Mechanism: Yield Stripping

**Yield-bearing asset → Principal Token (PT) + Yield Token (YT)**

- **Principal Token (PT):** Represents the right to redeem the underlying principal at maturity, subject to the vault's redemption rules.
- **Yield Token (YT):** Represents entitlement to yield generated by the underlying asset over a defined maturity period, subject to protocol accounting and settlement rules.

Separating these rights allows principal and future yield to become distinct instruments with independent trading strategies.

## 4. MVP Objective

The MVP will validate Rhea Strip's fundamental mechanism through one complete and easy-to-understand user journey.

**MVP goal:** Demonstrate how a user deposits stNEAR, receives PT and YT, trades either exposure independently, and settles principal and yield at maturity.

The MVP is a first implementation of the broader Rhea Strip protocol—not a standalone staking-yield-selling application. Forward-selling yield is a flagship use case built on top of PT/YT separation.

## 5. MVP Core Flow

### Flow at a Glance

**Select Asset → Deposit → Strip Yield → Receive PT + YT → Trade → Maturity & Settlement**

### Step 1 — Select a Yield-Bearing Asset

The user selects an asset to tokenize.

**MVP underlying:** Meta Pool stNEAR.

The protocol is designed to support additional yield-bearing assets:

- stNEAR — Meta Pool
- LiNEAR
- RHEA LP
- USDY / BUIDL through NEAR Intents

The user chooses the amount to deposit and an available maturity series.

### Step 2 — Deposit & Yield Stripping

The user deposits stNEAR into the Rhea Strip vault.

The vault accounts for the underlying principal and its yield. The protocol then separates the position into two instruments:

- **PT:** Represents the user's principal redemption rights at maturity.
- **YT:** Represents the future-yield entitlement for the selected maturity period.

The user receives PT and YT according to the protocol's issuance and accounting rules. An underlying position that previously combined principal and yield is now represented by two separate financial instruments.

### Step 3 — Independent PT/YT Trading

After stripping, the user can manage PT and YT independently.

#### PT Market

Users can buy or sell PT to manage principal exposure. Buying PT below its maturity redemption value can create a fixed-yield opportunity, subject to price, redemption terms, and risks.

#### YT Market

Users can buy or sell YT to gain or reduce exposure to future yield.

**Flagship MVP use case: Forward-Selling Yield**

A YT holder sells future-yield entitlement to a buyer in exchange for liquidity upfront, instead of waiting for the yield period to finish.

The buyer acquires the yield entitlement represented by the YT, subject to protocol rules. This makes future yield a tradable financial instrument rather than only passive asset income.

### Step 4 — Maturity & Settlement

At maturity, the protocol settles principal and yield rights based on each holder's position:

- PT holders can redeem the underlying principal according to maturity and vault redemption rules.
- YT holders can claim accrued yield attributable to their entitlement.
- YT expires after the relevant yield period has been settled.

This step validates that PT/YT separation is supported by enforceable accounting and settlement—not merely token issuance.

## 6. MVP Feature Scope

### Core Features

| Feature | MVP Scope |
|---|---|
| Yield Asset Integration | Meta Pool stNEAR as the first underlying |
| Yield Vault | Underlying custody/accounting and yield tracking |
| PT/YT Tokenization | Separate principal and future-yield rights |
| Maturity Series | One initial maturity series |
| PT Redemption | Principal redemption at maturity |
| YT Claim | Claim yield according to entitlement |
| PT Trading | Independent principal-exposure trading |
| YT Trading | Independent future-yield trading |
| Forward Yield Selling | Sell future yield for upfront liquidity |
| Portfolio | PT/YT balances, yield accrual, and maturity status |

### Protocol Compatibility

The protocol architecture remains extensible to additional assets. Asset expansion does not change the fundamental yield-stripping, PT/YT separation, or maturity-settlement model.

- **Liquid staking assets:** stNEAR, LiNEAR
- **Liquidity-provider assets:** RHEA LP
- **Tokenized RWA:** USDY, BUIDL
- **Cross-chain onboarding:** NEAR Intents

These integrations are part of the protocol vision; they are not all required for the MVP demo.

## 7. Target Users

- **Liquid Staking Holders:** Users seeking greater flexibility over principal and staking-yield exposure.
- **Yield Traders:** Users trading future yield or taking positions on yield changes.
- **DAO Treasuries:** Organizations seeking flexible yield management and fixed-yield strategies.
- **RWA Investors:** Users seeking separate principal and yield exposure in tokenized real-world assets.

## 8. Value Proposition

### 1. Make Yield Tradable
Future yield becomes a separate instrument that can be priced and traded.

### 2. Separate Principal and Yield
Users can manage principal and future-yield exposure independently.

### 3. Enable New Financial Strategies
PT/YT markets support fixed-yield positioning, yield speculation, and forward-selling future yield.

## 9. MVP Success Criteria

The MVP is successful when a user can:

1. Deposit stNEAR into the yield vault.
2. Receive PT and YT through yield stripping.
3. Hold and trade PT and YT independently.
4. Sell future yield through YT trading.
5. Redeem principal and claim eligible yield at maturity.

These capabilities demonstrate the core of Rhea Strip: converting yield-bearing assets into separately tradable principal and yield instruments.

## 10. Future Expansion

Following validation of the core mechanism, Rhea Strip can expand into:

- Multi-asset yield tokenization.
- Multiple PT/YT maturity markets.
- Time-decaying AMM for PT/YT trading.
- Cross-chain asset onboarding via NEAR Intents.
- RWA yield tokenization.
- Advanced interest-rate derivatives.
- Protocol incentives and liquidity programs.

## 11. Closing Statement

Rhea Strip introduces a new financial primitive to NEAR: the separation of principal and future yield into two independently tradable instruments.

By validating one underlying asset and one complete user journey, the MVP establishes the foundation for a multi-asset yield-tokenization and interest-rate-derivatives protocol across NEAR and cross-chain assets.
